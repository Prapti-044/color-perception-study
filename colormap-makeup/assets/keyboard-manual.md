# Guidleines for performing the tasks

Welcome! This guide will help you understand how to participate in the color perception study.

## 1. What You See
- You will see scatterplot stimuli on the screen for each trial.
- Your task is to decide whether the pair of colored points are the **same** or **different**.



## 2. How to Respond
- Press **`f`** or click **Same** button → if the two plots are the **same** color.  
- Press **`j`** or click **Different** button → if the two plots are **different** colors.

<img src="colormap-makeup/assets/keyboard.png" alt="Keyboard" width="450" height="450">

## 3. Moving to the Next Stimuli
After making your choice:
- Press the **Enter** key, **or**  
- Click the **Next** button  

Both will advance you to the next trial.
