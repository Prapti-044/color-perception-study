# Help - Color Perception Study Guide

## Study Overview

Welcome to our color perception research study! This experiment investigates how individuals perceive and interpret colors across various visual stimuli.

## What to Expect

The study consists of several phases designed to systematically analyze your color perception abilities:

### Phase 1: Setup and Training
- **Consent Form**: Review and agree to participate in the study
- **Introduction**: Learn about the study objectives and procedures
- **Color Training**: Complete 3 practice tasks to familiarize yourself with color comparison questions

### Phase 2: Color Perception Tasks
You'll view multiple scatterplot visualizations and answer whether two colored points appear the same or different. 

### Phase 3: Attention Checks
Throughout the study, you'll encounter simple attention check questions (like counting letters in words) to ensure you're paying attention.

### Phase 4: Background Questionnaires
- **Demographics**: Age, gender, education, etc.
- **Color Theory Knowledge**: Questions about your experience with color theory
- **Makeup Experience**: Questions about your familiarity and use of makeup products

## How to Respond

### For Color Comparison Tasks:
- Look carefully at the two colored points in each scatterplot
- Decide whether they appear to be the **same color** or **different colors**
- Select your answer using the radio buttons in the sidebar
- Click **Next** to proceed to the next task

## Important Guidelines

### Study Integrity:
- Answer based on your own perception - there are no "right" or "wrong" answers for most tasks
- Some tasks may seem repetitive - this is intentional for research purposes

### Navigation:
- Use the **Next** button to advance through tasks
- Progress is shown in the progress bar at the top
- You cannot go back to previous questions once submitted
